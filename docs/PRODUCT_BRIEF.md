# Product brief

RADARKit is a modular creative-operations and media-utility toolkit for RADARCharts by REM. It gives internal teams one place to prepare media, coordinate releases, research music data, organize campaign assets, and produce platform-native campaign material without turning every workflow into a bespoke tool.

## Target users

The first users are authorized RADARCharts operators, artist-development collaborators, content editors, release coordinators, and campaign managers. The product assumes controlled access rather than public self-registration.

## Product principles

RADARKit should be modular, rights-aware, mobile-usable, and operationally clear. Every department should have a defined purpose and reusable UI patterns. Official APIs and user-authorized access take priority over brittle or unsupported techniques. Private operational data must remain separate from the public source repository.

## Departments

Media Lab handles authorized intake, artwork, metadata preparation, and organization. Playlist Studio handles importing, matching, transfer, backup, and synchronization only where officially supported. Social Studio organizes captions, hooks, calls to action, hashtags, editorial content, and campaign presets. Its first browser-only slice also turns a local featured image, article title, and excerpt into a full-bleed 9:16 story asset with PNG export; it does not connect accounts or publish. Converter Lab covers image and audio conversion, resizing, compression, trimming, and metadata management. Research Desk covers artist, song, release, credits, ISRC, release-date, and platform-link research. Asset Desk organizes campaign assets through folders, tags, naming conventions, metadata, and export history.

## Scope boundaries

Foundation does not implement intensive media processing, platform mutations, authentication, or production deployment. DRM circumvention, stream ripping, watermark removal, unauthorized redistribution, and access-control bypass are explicitly outside the product boundary.

## Future opportunities

Smart Crop, Cover Inspector, Music Link Collector, Bio Generator, Press Release Builder, Video Thumbnail Maker, Audio Metadata Editor, QR Generator, File Compressor, and Text Extractor can be added as independent modules after the core security and processing seams are proven.
